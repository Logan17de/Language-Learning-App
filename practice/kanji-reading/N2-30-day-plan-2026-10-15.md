## Daily operating model
- Days 1-4: diagnostic retrieval and rapid cleanup of N5/N4 weak items; start N3/N2 every day.
- Days 5-20: high-volume first exposure for remaining N3/N2 and uncovered lower-level words; compounds and kana+kanji in every session.
- Days 21-26: close all remaining coverage gaps, while emphasizing failed items and speed.
- Days 27-30: timed reading, delayed retrieval, context stories, error-list closure. New discoveries still get tracked.
- Provisional first-pass pace: at least 50 distinct kana+kanji entries per day over first 26 days (1,300 exposure slots) PLUS a separately counted daily compound quota after catalog inventory is known. Existing mastered words can be verified efficiently; weak and novel words need more time.
- Split daily work into several blocks, not a single rapid recognition drill. Review due items every day in addition to new words.
- Word coverage means *seen and tested*, not guaranteed mastered. Track reviewed, correct, missed and still-untested separately.

## Evaluation
- Test reading in kana and English meaning; store them independently.
- Wrong whole word => that exact target weak/critical according to measured accuracy; do not automatically demote its constituent individual kanji.
- Mark hesitation as familiar/slow retrieval and observe latency when available.
- Use per-word attempts, correctness, last seen, last correct, last incorrect, confidence and forgetting trend after enough evidence exists.
- Verified status thresholds: mastered >=90% with >=5 attempts; familiar 70-89%; weak 40-69%; critical <40%. Untested is UNASSESSED/UNKNOWN, not automatically critical.
- Review allocation when evidence permits: weak 50%, critical 25%, familiar 15%, mastered 10%. Introduce unassessed vocabulary as a separate coverage quota.
- Missed: recheck after 1, 3, 7 and 14 sessions. Repeatedly correct: 7, 14, 30 sessions. Interleave at least 3 other targets before repeating any missed item.

## Material and safety
- Use APK-derived kanji-level-index.json whitelist. Filter out N1 characters even when an app entry is filed under N2.
- Prioritize historically problematic exact targets: 特色, 休業, 用紙, 飲料, 屋外, 下りる, 走り出す.
- Contrasting readings and visually similar compounds are valuable; don't substitute character drills for whole-word testing.
- Contextual 10-line Japanese stories after sessions should use only allowed N5-N2 kanji.
- Keep all original app export files immutable. Update progress.json only on explicit feedback; don't fabricate completed sessions.
- Commit completed practice feedback with message: `kanji-progress: update stats after practice YYYY-MM-DD`.
