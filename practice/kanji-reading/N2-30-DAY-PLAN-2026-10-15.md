## Exercise design and assessment
- Focus on **whole vocabulary entries** (compounds and kana+kanji), with exact target key and reading + English meaning assessed separately. A wrong `compound|特色` must **not** downgrade `single|特` or `single|色` without independent evidence.
- Interleave N5, N4, N3, N2 with weak items rotated apart. Never introduce N1 kanji as newly tested material.
- Starting item mix: about 50% observed weak, 25% previously unknown/new (not called critical without attempt statistics), 15% familiar, and 10% mastered. Rebalance after more evidence.
- For words with sufficient performance records, use classifications: Mastered >=90% and >=5 appearances; Familiar 70–89%; Weak 40–69%; Critical <40%. If insufficient evidence, keep source state's vocabulary label and mark metrics provisional, not fabricated.
- Correct reading and meaning are independent; partial credit must not create invented full correctness.
- Missed-card review checkpoints: +1, +3, +7, +14 completed sessions. Correct-repeat intervals: +7, +14, +30 sessions. Day-number is not a substitute for session-number.
- After each completed session: grade every answer; report score/accuracy/strongest/weakest/forgetting risk/recommended review/story/next focus; update the appropriate vocabulary and context evidence in `progress.json`, and for app-connected cards optionally `app-sync/overrides.json` with new revisions; commit using `kanji-progress: update stats after practice YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Avoid claiming live synchronization until `app-sync/current.json` is present and inspected.
