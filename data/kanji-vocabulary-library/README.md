# JLPT study collection

One combined collection, organized by level and category.

Each of `N5/`, `N4/`, `N3/`, `N2/` and `N1/` contains:

- `kanji.json`: the saved kanji study words, including compounds and kanji-with-kana.
- `vocabs.json`: additional vocabulary not already in the kanji file.

**9,150 unique written entries; zero duplicates across levels or categories.**

Each written entry appears once. Alternate readings, meanings, examples and study notes are merged inside it. Kanji-category entries take priority when a word also appears in vocabulary; the earliest applicable level is retained. Source-specific folders and per-entry source labels are omitted.

This is a content snapshot. Personal ratings, flags, review history, settings and credentials are not included. The local flashcard DB is unchanged.
